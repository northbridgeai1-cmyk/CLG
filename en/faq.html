The questions people ask before they start

Direct answers, including the ones where the honest answer is "it depends." If yours isn't here, ask the assistant or send us a message on WhatsApp.

I need a US visa. Where do I start?

Not by picking a visa. The first step is understanding your full profile: your profession or business, the capital you actually have available, your family situation and your timeline. We design a strategy around that profile rather than selling individual filings. The free Brújula CLG takes two minutes and is the usual starting point.

My children want to study in the United States. What are my options?

It depends on which path you take first. If you immigrate through a professional or investment route, your minor children can generally accompany you as dependents. There are also direct student visas and, in some cases, a family petition. Which one is cheaper and faster depends on your own situation, which is why it gets decided in the strategy phase rather than assumed at the start.

Does CLG sell investment visas like the E-2 or the EB-5?

No. We don't sell visas or residency. We design the full strategy that makes them possible: the business, the capital, the documentation and the legal filing, and we stay with the case until it's resolved.

How long does the process take?

It varies by pathway and by case. Rather than quote a generic number, the Evaluación CLG delivers a realistic time range in writing, based on your documents and the route we're recommending.

What happens if my case is denied?

No serious firm can guarantee an outcome, and you should be wary of one that does. What we control is the quality of the file: complete documentation, solid evidence, and a proper response to any RFE. If your profile isn't strong for a given pathway, we tell you during the assessment, before you spend money moving forward.

Can I keep running my business at home while the process is underway?

In many cases yes, depending on the pathway. It's one of the things settled during the Design phase of the CLG Method, around your real situation rather than a template.

Do I have to move immediately once I'm approved?

Not necessarily. Some pathways let you plan the timing of the move within certain legal windows. We review it case by case.

What happens to my spouse and children's status?

In most business and professional pathways, a spouse and minor children can accompany you as dependents, with the possibility of work or study authorization depending on the category.

Why work with CLG and not another firm?

Because we don't sell an isolated filing. We look at the whole spectrum of your case, business, profession, family, capital and timing, before recommending a pathway. You get an attorney and a case manager assigned by name, and nearly twenty years of designing strategies for clients across Latin America.

How much does it cost?

We publish real ranges by visa type on the fees page, so you know what to expect before the first conversation instead of after it.

My case is complicated. Can you still help?

Complex cases, multi-entity structures, an unconventional source of funds, or a prior denial, are exactly where the CLG Method earns its keep. They get examined in depth during the Evaluación CLG.

Do I need to travel to the United States to begin?

No. The assessment and much of the process can begin over WhatsApp or video call. We also hold in-person sessions in several countries across Latin America.

My US company needs to hire talent from abroad. Can you help?

Yes. We work with companies already established in the United States that need to bring key people in from abroad, designing the full transfer strategy through pathways like the L-1.

Is the United States not your only priority?

We also talk about options in other jurisdictions, including the UAE and Spain — message us to explore it.
