What people ask at exactly this step

Is the CLG Assessment refundable?

Our guarantee isn't "if you don't qualify we refund you." It's that we always find a route. If you qualify to move now, that's your Assessment, and it's credited in full against your fee. If you don't qualify yet, you leave with a written roadmap rather than a refund. We return the payment in full only when no route exists at all, now or in the future, which is uncommon.

What if my profile wouldn't qualify today?

We don't leave you with a no. You get the Immigration Roadmap regardless, with the concrete steps to get there — capital, time, credentials, whatever it takes — and a realistic timeline.

Do I need to speak to someone before paying?

No. You can book your Assessment right here. If you have a quick question about the process, WhatsApp is available the whole time, though it isn't a substitute for the analysis of your case.

How long does it take after I pay?

The team contacts you to schedule your 90-minute strategy session and begin reviewing your documents.
