The routes we work with

These are the categories we file most often.

E-2 Business owners and investors

Invest in and run your own business in the United States. There's no fixed minimum in the law, but the investment has to be substantial and the business has to actually operate. Only for nationals of treaty countries.

See E-2 →
L-1 Companies and executives

Transfer executive, managerial or specialized staff between your company abroad and your US operation. Also the route if your company is already here and needs to bring people in.

See L-1 →
EB-5 Investors

A larger investment that creates jobs and leads straight to permanent residency for you and your family. The minimum adjusts for inflation from 1 January 2027.

See EB-5 →
O-1 Artists, athletes and standout talent

Live and work practicing your discipline, based on a demonstrable track record and a sponsor or agent. It doesn't require mass fame. It requires evidence.

See O-1 →
EB-2 NIW Professionals

Permanent residency with no job offer and no labor certification, when your work has substantial merit and national importance to the United States.

See EB-2 NIW →
Family Families

Family petitions, your children's schooling and long-term residency planning. Timelines vary a great deal by relationship and country of origin.

See family routes →

The CLG Method™

Four phases, and a fifth after approval.

01

Diagnosis

We review your whole profile: profession, business, corporate structure and source of funds.

02

Design

We pick a primary route and a backup, then structure the capital, the company and the calendar.

03

Documentation

We build the file: business plan, incorporation, letters, financials, credentials.

04

Filing

We file, prepare you for the interview and respond to any RFE.

The fifth phase is Continuidad CLG, for clients with an approved case: renewals on a calendar, with your family and company inside the same file. $950 USD a year.
