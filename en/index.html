Invest in and run your own business in the United States. No fixed minimum amount, but the investment has to be substantial and real.

Frequently asked

What people ask us most

What can I do with a visa that I can't do without one?

Without a visa, the United States is a place you visit. With the right one it becomes a place where your life works: your company operates, your children study, your spouse works, your career moves. Goals CLG answers this goal by goal.

I need a visa for the US. Where do I start?

Not by picking a visa. By understanding your full profile: profession or business, capital, family and timing. The Brújula CLG points you in a direction free, in two minutes, and the Evaluación CLG is the formal analysis with an attorney.

What exactly does the Brújula CLG include, and why is it free?

It's an initial orientation: which pathways your profile points to and what you should look at first. It's free because it's orientation, not a file review. The full analysis, with documents, a written report and a session with an attorney, is the Evaluación CLG, and it does cost money, credited back to your fee if you move forward.

Does CLG sell investment visas like the E-2 or EB-5?

No. CLG doesn't sell visas or residency. It designs the full strategy that makes them possible, covering the business, the capital, the documentation and the legal filing, and stays with the case until it's resolved.

My US company needs to hire talent from abroad. Can you help?

Yes. We work with companies already established in the United States that need to bring key people in from abroad, through pathways like the L-1.

Is the United States not your only priority?

We also talk about options in other jurisdictions, including the UAE and Spain. See Global Mobility.
