Continuidad CLG · After approval

Your case is closed. Your strategy isn't.

You've been through Diagnosis, Design, Documentation and Filing. Continuidad CLG is the next turn of the same wheel: someone keeps looking after your file now that you've got what you came for.

Visas renew. Families grow. Businesses hire. Every one of those touches your immigration status, and it costs far less to handle them on a calendar than to solve them in an emergency.

What's included

Seven concrete things. None of them a sales call.

Annual compliance review. We keep the renewal calendar so you don't have to.
Priority access for quick questions, ahead of the new-client queue.
Family updates at a reduced rate. A child born abroad or a change in your spouse's status gets handled inside your existing file, without opening a new case from scratch.
Preferential rates on additional filings, like adding an employee under your existing E-2 structure or converting category later on.
Two free sessions a year with outside professionals. An accountant for tax or payroll, an HR specialist to prepare interviews, or whoever your situation calls for.
A monthly community call, to stay connected with other CLG clients and hear about the rest of the ecosystem first-hand, including Global Expansion 360.
Priority handling if an RFE arrives. To be clear: the RFE fee is still quoted separately, as always. What changes is that your case goes to the front of the queue rather than the general tray.
