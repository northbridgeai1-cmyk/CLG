Questions from families

Frequently asked

My children want to study in the United States. What are my options?

If you immigrate first through a professional or investment route, your children under 21 can come with you as dependents. There are also direct student visas and, in some cases, a family petition.

What is the diversity visa lottery?

An annual program awarding up to 55,000 residency visas by random selection to people from countries with historically low immigration to the US. Eligibility depends on your country of birth and your education or work experience.

Can I plan without moving right away?

Yes. Many families build their strategy years in advance, with no intention of moving immediately, so the option is there when they want it.
