Goals CLG

Don't search by visa type. Search by what you want to achieve.

Nearly every immigration site is organized by category: E-2, O-1, EB-5. As though you arrived already knowing which one you need. Almost nobody does. So this page runs the other way round, starting from what you want to be true in your life.

We know the law. What we actually care about is your outcome. Your child at that university. Your business running. Your family together. The visa is the mechanism. Your life is the point.

The question underneath

What can you do with a visa that you can't do without one?

Without a visa, the United States is a place you visit. With the right one it becomes a place where your life works: your company operates, your children study, your spouse works, your career moves. Here's the answer, goal by goal.

How can my child study in the United States?

What becomes possible: your child can enroll full time at any school or university in the country, live there while they study, and work on a limited basis on campus.

How it's done: usually an F-1 student visa. If the whole family is moving together, they can come in as a dependent within a wider family strategy.

How do I open or buy a business in the United States?

What becomes possible: you can found, buy or operate a real business in the United States. And live there to run it yourself, rather than financing it from a distance.

How it's done: usually an E-2 visa, which asks for a substantial investment with no fixed minimum. For larger investments, the EB-5 also leads to permanent residency.

Can I bring my family with me?

What becomes possible: in most cases, yes. Your spouse and children under 21 can come with you and live with you. In several categories your spouse can work too.

How it's done: as derivative status on your own visa. It isn't a separate filing, it comes inside the same case.

My company is already in the US. How do I hire talent from abroad?

What becomes possible: you can bring key people over from your operation abroad, whether executive, managerial or specialized, instead of being limited to hiring locally.

How it's done: usually an L-1 structure, which allows transferring staff between a related company abroad and your US entity.

I'm an artist or an athlete. How do I build my career in the US?

What becomes possible: you can live and work in the United States practicing your discipline. Competing, exhibiting, performing, recording. Without needing a traditional full-time employer.

How it's done: usually an O-1 visa for standout track records, with a sponsor or agent.

Can I eventually apply for permanent residency?

What becomes possible: depends on the route. Some lead straight to the green card. Others are an intermediate step you build from later.

How it's done: EB-5 and EB-2 NIW go direct. E-2 and O-1 don't automatically, though many clients use them as the first step of a multi-year strategy.
