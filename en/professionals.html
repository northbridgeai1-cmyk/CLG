Routes we assess

From your field to the right category

EB-2 NIW

National Interest Waiver

Permanent residency with no job offer and no labor certification, if your work carries substantial merit and national importance. Built for advanced-degree professionals.

O-1 / EB-1

Extraordinary Ability

For artists, athletes, scientists and executives with demonstrable recognition in their field. The O-1 is temporary; the EB-1 can lead directly to permanent residency.

EB-3 / H-3 / TN

Other professional routes

Questions from professionals

Frequently asked

What is the EB-2 NIW and who can apply?

It allows permanent residency with no job offer and no labor certification, if your work has substantial merit and national importance to the United States. It applies to advanced-degree professionals and people of exceptional ability.

Is the O-1 only for famous artists?

No. It requires solid evidence of recognition in your field — awards, publications, selective memberships, critical roles — not mass fame.

Do I need a US job offer?

It depends on the pathway. The EB-2 NIW doesn't require one. Other categories do, or benefit from it. We assess this in your Evaluación CLG.
