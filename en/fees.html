What getting it wrong costs

Your legal fee is usually a small fraction of the capital you're already committing to the business or the career. A denial doesn't just cost the filing. It costs months, a refusal on your record that follows every future application, and a business already capitalized and waiting.

The question isn't whether the fee is high. It's whether saving on it is worth risking everything else.
