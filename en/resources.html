EB-5 · August 2026

New DHS proposed rule

It's a proposal, not final law, and the process usually takes more than a year. It does signal where USCIS is heading, so it's worth planning with that in mind.

EB-5 · January 2027

Minimum investment adjusts for inflation

The automatic adjustment takes effect from 1 January 2027. If you're seriously weighing EB-5, the calendar matters.
